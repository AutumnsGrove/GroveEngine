# What are Sparks?

Some days the words are already there, waiting. Other days the page just stares back. Sparks are a small nudge for the second kind of day — a bank of writing prompts that show up right when a blank draft needs one.

## How it works

Open a new post in [[flow|Flow]] with nothing written yet, and a Spark card appears above the editor: a short prompt, tagged by mood and length, ready to use or ignore.

- **Another spark** shuffles to a different prompt if the first one doesn't land
- **Use this** pins the prompt — it becomes a small reference strip that stays visible while you write, so you can glance back at it without losing your place
- The small **x** minimizes the card into a pill in the corner, in case you want it out of the way for a moment. It's never gone for good — click the pill to bring it back

Once you've typed a title or started writing, the chooser card gets out of the way on its own. Sparks is a starting point, not a permanent fixture — it only shows up while the page is genuinely blank.

## Nothing is inferred

Using a Spark is always something you choose, not something Grove assumes. Typing past a prompt that's showing doesn't quietly attribute your post to it — only clicking **Use this** does. If you pin a prompt and change your mind, you can unpin it just as easily.

## Turning Sparks off

Not everyone wants a prompt on the way in. If you'd rather start every draft with a blank page and nothing else, head to [[arbor|Arbor]] → **Settings → Features** and switch Sparks off. It's a single toggle, on by default, and takes effect the next time you start a new post.
